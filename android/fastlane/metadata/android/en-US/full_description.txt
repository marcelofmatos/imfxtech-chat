### Messaging how it should be

Whether for your friends, your company or local community, IMFxTech Chat supports a variety of features to cover all your daily needs.

#### Easy to use

Simply install, log in and start chatting. The intuitive user interface makes secure communication as easy as possible.

#### Secure

With olm/megolm end-to-end encryption, encrypted message backups and an automated key trust system, IMFxTech Chat is among the most secure messengers available.

#### Decentral

Based on the open Matrix protocol, IMFxTech Chat is part of a decentralized messaging network which no single entity can control. Host your own chat server while remaining in contact with everyone.

### Feature rich and reliable

From public chats with tens of thousands of participants and polls to custom stickers, spaces, rich moderation tools, and multi-device support, IMFxTech Chat delivers a comprehensive communication suite.

#### Not another walled garden

IMFxTech Chat does not lock you into another walled garden! It is fully compatible with any other Matrix client like Element, Nheko, Cinny or NeoChat. Learn more at matrix.org.
